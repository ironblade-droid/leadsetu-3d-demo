v4 additions (retrieved 13 November 2026, Asia/Calcutta):

- marblevein_*  ambientCG Marble001, CC0
  https://ambientcg.com/a/Marble001
  1K JPEG Color, NormalGL and Roughness. Used for the Marble flooring option
  (white slab with visible grey-green veining). The earlier Poly Haven marble_01
  tiled limestone remains on disk as marble_*.jpg but is unused in v4.

- fabric_nor.jpg, fabric_rough.jpg  Poly Haven Fabric Pattern 07, CC0
  https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07  by Rob Tuytel
  1K JPEG OpenGL normal and roughness (cotton weave). The gingham colour maps
  are not used — sofa, bed, curtains and mattress are tinted by style and
  take weave from these two maps.

- brushed_nor.jpg, brushed_rough.jpg, brushed_diff.jpg, brushed_metal.jpg
  ambientCG Metal032, CC0
  https://ambientcg.com/a/Metal032
  1K JPEG NormalGL and Roughness used for brushed brass and matte-black
  hardware (colour comes from the material tint). Albedo and metalness maps
  are on disk but unused.
